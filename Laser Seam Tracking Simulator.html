About this model
  • This is a teaching model, not a calibrated process simulation. Weld position error is the lateral distance between the seam and the laser spot, and it must stay within ±0.3 mm.
  • The sensor projects a laser line across the lap joint. The upper panel is higher than the lower one, so the line is not in one straight line across the two panels. The sideways position of that jog is the seam position. The jog height drawn here is for clarity.
  • Travel speed is 60 mm/s over a 240 mm seam, welding upward. The camera runs at 200 frames per second and looks ahead of the weld spot. A pre-scan finds the seam start before welding.
  • Each frame passes through image processing and communication (60 ms), a median filter over five frames, and a lead of one robot lag (40 ms). The lateral axis then follows the corrected point.
  • The sideways scale in the close-up is stretched so that sub-millimetre errors are visible. The seam disturbances are examples, not measured data.
